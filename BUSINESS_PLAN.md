# eVault Business Plan

## 1. Problem Statement
Legal records, court proceedings, and evidentiary documents suffer from fragmentation, loss, and tampering. Courts rely on aging databases that lack cryptographic integrity, resulting in trust deficits and inefficient verification processes.

## 2. Value Proposition
eVault provides a unified, blockchain-backed legal document vault. By separating the encrypted document payload (stored securely off-chain) from the immutable cryptographic hash (stored on Hyperledger Fabric), eVault guarantees data integrity without sacrificing speed or scalability. 

## 3. Target Users
- **Courts & Judges**: Need to quickly verify that a piece of evidence or a filing has not been altered since upload.
- **Lawyers**: Require a secure platform to upload case files, share them with opposing counsel or clients, and maintain an indisputable audit trail.
- **Clients / Citizens**: Gain transparent, controlled access to their case files without relying on paper trails.

## 4. Market Opportunity & Impact
- **Access to Justice**: Citizens can access verified copies of their documents instantly.
- **Efficiency**: Reduces time spent authenticating documents during court proceedings.
- **Institutional Procurement**: Target B2G (Business-to-Government) sales models, where regional courts adopt eVault as a SaaS or private-cloud deployment.

## 5. Adoption Model & Revenue
- **SaaS Licensing**: Tiered licensing for law firms based on storage usage.
- **Enterprise Contracts**: On-premise or managed private-cloud deployments for High Courts and Supreme Courts.
- **API Monetization**: Charging third-party legal tech platforms to interface with the eVault Verification API.
